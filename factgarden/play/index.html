About this page

I built Fact Garden for my daughter. The levels, colors and timer settings were chosen for her. Her copy has a sign-in and saves progress to my server, so it follows her between devices and I can see which facts she keeps missing.

This page is a public demo with no sign-in, and nothing is sent to the server. Progress is saved only in this browser, and is lost if you clear your browsing data, use a private window, or switch browsers or devices. On an iPad or iPhone, Safari also deletes it after seven days of browsing without a visit here. Adding the page to the home screen prevents that.
